Terms and Conditions

Please review these terms before creating an account or placing a pickup order through ISTILO.

Owner review neededCancellation, refund, and unavailable-item policies have not been provided by the shop. This page avoids inventing these policies; the owner should confirm them before treating this draft as final.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By creating an account or placing an order, you confirm that you have read and agree to these Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy. You must select the required agreement checkbox to submit account registration or an online order.

2. Ordering Process

Online orders are for pickup. You must be signed in to a verified customer account. Select available products, options, and quantities, review the displayed total, and submit your order. The name and phone number on your account are used for the order; optional order notes may be included.

Submitting an order creates an order record and displays an order confirmation. For online orders, payment proof must be submitted and verified by the shop before the crew prepares the order.

3. Product Availability

Products and options are subject to availability. The system checks availability when an order is submitted and may prevent an order for an unavailable item. Items are not automatically substituted. The shop’s process if availability changes after submission needs owner confirmation.

4. Pricing and Payment

Prices and totals are displayed in Philippine pesos and are based on the products, options, and quantities selected. Online orders currently offer GCash or Maya. Payment is made outside the ordering website; customers submit proof for shop verification. The website does not process an e-wallet transfer.

5. Order Confirmation and Cancellation

The on-screen confirmation means the order was submitted; it does not mean payment has been verified or preparation has begun. Crew accepts an online order for preparation after payment verification.

The website does not provide a customer-side cancellation control. Use the Contact section to ask the shop about an order. Cancellation, refund, and adjustment terms have not been supplied and must be confirmed by the owner; no refund guarantee or timeline is stated here.

6. Customer Responsibilities

Keep your account details accurate, protect your sign-in credentials, choose the correct items and options, and review the quantity, payment method, and total before submitting. Provide order notes that are relevant to preparing your pickup order.

7. Privacy and Data Protection

Account, order, inquiry, and payment-proof information is handled as described in the Privacy Policy. Review that page before submitting information. Details that require owner confirmation are identified there.

8. System Availability

The ordering website may occasionally be unavailable or experience delays. If a submission does not display a confirmation, check your tracked orders before submitting again. Uninterrupted access is not guaranteed.

9. Changes to Terms and Conditions

ISTILO may update this page as its ordering process or policies change. The current version will be available at this address. Please review it when creating an account or placing an order.

10. Contact Information

For questions about these terms or an order, use the Contact section of the ISTILO ordering website. The shop owner should confirm official contact details and any additional support process.
