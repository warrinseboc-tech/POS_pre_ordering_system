Customer information

Privacy Policy

This draft describes information the ISTILO ordering system handles and how it is used. It should be reviewed by the shop owner against the shop’s complete privacy practices.

Owner verification requiredThe system does not specify a data-retention period, an official privacy contact or data protection officer, or all hosting and service-provider disclosures. The shop owner should confirm these details and the privacy-request process before final publication.

Information the system handles

  • Customer account: name, email address, phone number, and a password stored as a password hash.
  • Orders: selected products and options, quantities, order notes, order status, and selected payment method.
  • Payment proof: receipt image, transaction reference, amount, and review status when you submit a payment proof.
  • Messages: name, email address, topic, and message submitted through the Contact form.
  • Website operation: a session identifier and security token for session handling and request protection. Notification read-status is stored in your browser.

How information is used

Account and order information supports customer sign-in, order submission and tracking, order updates, and shop order handling. Payment details and receipt images are used to verify an online payment. Contact-form information is saved to the shop’s inquiry inbox so the shop can handle your message.

Who can access information

Order information is available to authorized shop crew and administrators for order handling; inquiry messages are available to administrators through the shop inbox. The shop’s hosting, database, and service-provider arrangements need confirmation by the owner before this notice is final.

Payments

Online orders currently offer GCash and Maya. Payment is made outside the website; the site asks you to submit proof for shop review. Do not enter your e-wallet password or sign-in credentials into the ordering website.

Retention and your requests

The system does not publish a data-retention period or self-service data deletion process. To ask a question or make a request about your information, use the Contact section. The shop owner must confirm the official request process and any applicable retention requirements.

Changes and contact

This draft may be updated when the shop confirms its privacy practices. For questions, use the Contact section of the ISTILO ordering website. An official privacy contact should be added after confirmation by the shop owner.
